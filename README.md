# Personalized-Finance-Assistant
Stock Market Analytics with Agentic AI

## Project Problem Statement -
 
Personalized Finance Assistant is designed to address the pain point of fragmented, time-consuming financial research by providing financial professionals, analysts, and investors with a unified platform for real-time, AI-powered insights and concise answers. Motivated by the need to synthesize data instantly from diverse sources like market prices, earnings reports, and news, its purpose is to streamline decision-making and eliminate the manual aggregation of information.
